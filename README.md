# NIST CSF 2.0 Gap Analysis — Cova Funds

**Analyst:** Nancy Okereke | Cybersecurity Analyst  
**Date:** February 2026  
**Frameworks:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) 2.0

---

## Project Overview

This project documents a cybersecurity gap analysis conducted for Cova Funds, a fictional African fintech company offering mobile payments, wallet services, and small business lending. The assessment evaluates Cova Funds' current cybersecurity posture against all six functions of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) Version 2.0, identifies gaps, and provides prioritised recommendations.

---

## Company Profile

- **Company:** Cova Funds (fictional)
- **Industry:** Fintech
- **Size:** 50 employees
- **Operating Regions:** Nigeria (with planned expansion to Ghana and Kenya)
- **Data Processed:** Customer PII, transaction records, employee records

## Gap Analysis Summary

| # | Function | Abbreviation | Status | Priority |
|---|----------|-------------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Govern | GV | ❌ Gap | Critical |
| 2 | Identify | ID | ⚠️ Partial | High |
| 3 | Protect | PR | ⚠️ Partial | High |
| 4 | Detect | DE | ❌ Gap | Critical |
| 5 | Respond | RS | ❌ Gap | Critical |
| 6 | Recover | RC | ❌ Gap | High |

---

## Key Findings

- **4 of 6 functions** rated as Gap — no formal governance, detection, response, or recovery capabilities
- **2 of 6 functions** rated as Partial — basic asset awareness and access controls exist but are informal and undocumented
- Absence of an incident response plan creates direct regulatory exposure under NDPC (72-hour breach notification requirement)
- No SIEM or monitoring tools in place — security incidents could go undetected indefinitely
